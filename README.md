## Concept

A triptych, not a grid. One masthead names the day — **Thursday 19 November 2026** —
then three full-height panels carry the three biggest stories of the day, and a bottom
strip packs the rest of the world in by region. 29 stories in total: 3 heroes + 26 in the strip.

## The three panels

- **GTA VI LAUNCHES** — Metacritic 99, pre-orders top $1.5bn. The game launched worldwide
  on 19 November, midnight local time in every region.
- **SHENZHEN SUMMIT** — Trump, Xi and Putin meet for the first time. The first-ever
  trilateral summit of the three leaders, on the sidelines of APEC in Shenzhen.
- **ICJ RULES ON MYANMAR** — World court decides Rohingya case. The International Court
  of Justice delivers its judgment in Gambia v. Myanmar at The Hague.

## The strip

- **Americas:** US–Venezuela sign military deal; Fed minutes point to Dec hike; Jeffries
  picked for Speaker; Bukele helicopters hit Haiti gang villa; Trump: Iran eyes chemical arms.
- **Europe:** EU proposes €45bn loan for Ukraine; Ukraine hits Crimea bridge again; UK
  inflation rises to 3.6%; EU approves Indonesia trade deal; Germany cuts electricity tax.
- **Asia-Pacific:** Bangladesh unrest, Yunus home torched; IAEA: N. Korea expands Yongbyon;
  S. Korea Lee urges Pyongyang talks; Indonesia passes asset bill; Fiji disaster over drought.
- **Middle East:** Iran attacks Gulf states; Israel op beyond Gaza Yellow Line; Houthi
  missile aimed at Riyadh; COP31 ministers push climate talks.
- **Tech & Science:** Anthropic debuts on Nasdaq at $83; Voyager 1 reaches one light-day;
  NASA sets CRS-25 launch coverage; Deutsche Bank probed after file leak.
- **Markets:** Markets fall, FTSE down 1.3%; Van Gogh sells for record $190.4m; IEA: Hormuz
  crisis hits energy.

Every story was verified against reporting dated 19 November 2026. Where a story was
scheduled for the day, it is named as an event; no outcome is stated that did not happen.

## Run

`main.py` is stdlib-only. It copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`). No network, no third-party packages.

```
python3 main.py
```
